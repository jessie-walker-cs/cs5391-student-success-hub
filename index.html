The challenge

A real problem, a manageable project

Students in accelerated online programs may need to find the right university office, understand short instructional terms, and resolve technical or administrative problems across several systems. This prototype brings helpful navigation, planning, and support guidance into one accessible starting point.

You will work on a realistic software engineering problem without collecting actual student records or integrating with private university systems. The site is for learning and demonstration, not for replacing Texas State services.

What you will practice
  • Agile teamwork and professional communication
  • Requirements analysis and UML modeling
  • Three software development sprints
  • Unit, integration, system and accessibility testing
  • Documentation, demonstration and ethical reflection

Working together

Build your component. Connect to the whole.

Independent and testable

Each team delivers a component that can be demonstrated without the other two. Team size and implementation scope will reflect actual enrollment; an unselected team will not be assigned to another group.

Shared integration contract

Teams agree on navigation, resource IDs, sample JSON formats, accessibility expectations and integration points. They share interface specifications, not one another's graded solutions.
