How to sign up

  1. Review the project pages. Compare scope, required features, roles and interactions with other teams.
  2. Sign in to GitHub. You need a free account to create a signup issue.
  3. Open the subproject selection form. Choose exactly one team and briefly explain your interest. Use your GitHub username only.
  4. Submit by the deadline. Your GitHub Issue will display a creation timestamp. The instructor will review submissions and confirm team membership after the deadline.
  5. Remain with your team. Team changes after confirmation require instructor approval. Separate Canvas assignment instructions still apply.
Open GitHub signup form ↗

GitHub Issues are public. Do not provide your student ID, personal email, phone number, grades, or private information. The form opens only after the repository and Issues feature are enabled.

Need a private or accessible alternative?

Send your one-team selection directly to Dr. Walker through the Canvas Inbox by the same deadline. Do not post personal information publicly to explain why you need this option. Canvas is the official course communication system.
